**Cajun seasoning** ## Ingredients - 3 **table** spoons paprika - 2 **table** spoons garlic powder - 1 1/2 **table** spoons onion powder - 1 1/2 **table** spoons dried thyme - 1 1/2 **table** spoons dried oregano - 2 **tea** spoons black pepper - 1 1/2 to 2 **tea** spoons cayenne pepper # Preparation Mix and store in airtight container.
