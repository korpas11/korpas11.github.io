**Cajun seasoning** ## Ingredients - 3 tablespoons paprika - 2 tablespoons garlic powder - 1 1/2 tablespoons onion powder - 1 1/2 tablespoons dried thyme - 1 1/2 tablespoons dried oregano - 2 teaspoons black pepper - 1 1/2 to 2 teaspoons cayenne pepper # Preparation Mix and store in airtight container.
