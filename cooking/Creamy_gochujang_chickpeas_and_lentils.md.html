**Creamy gochujang chickpeas and lentils** ## Ingredients - 2 large poblano peppers - 1/4 teaspoon fine salt, plus more to taste - 3 large garlic cloves, minced - 2 tablespoon gochujang sauce - 1/4 cup tomato paste - 1 1/2 cup creamy oatmilk *(unsweetened, but sweetened is okay too)* - 1 1/2 teaspoon paprika - 2/3 cup dry chickpeas (for 2 cups of [cooked](Cooking_chickpeas.md.html)) - 2/3 cup dry lentils (for 2 cups of [cooked](Cooking_lentils.md.html)) - 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice ### For serving - Cooked rice - 1/4 cup chopped fresh spinach for garnish ## Preparation 1. In a large, deep saucepan combine 1/4 cup **water**, the **poblano pepper** and **salt** and cook over medium heat until the pepper becomes tender, about 3 minutes. 2. Add the garlic and cook for another minute. Add a splash of water if pan is dry. Add the **gochujang**, **tomato paste**, **oat milk**, and **paprika** and stir well to mix thoroughly. 3. Add the **cooked chickpeas** and **(cooked) lentils** and mix well. Bring to a gentle simmer, then reduce the heat to low, and cook until the sauce slightly thickened and the chickpeas are tender, about 10 minutes. Remove from heat and add the **lemon juice**. Taste and add more salt if needed. 4. Serve as is, or over rice or other grain. Add gochujang sauce on top if desired. Garnish with fresh greens. ## Source - [Vegan Wholesome: High-Protein Meals and Snacks to Energize and Nourish; A Cookbook](https://www.amazon.com/Vegan-Wholesome-High-Protein-Energize-Nourish-ebook/dp/B0D7GJSCVT)
