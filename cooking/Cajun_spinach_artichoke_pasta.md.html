**Cajun spinach artichoke pasta** ## Ingredients - fine sea salt - 2 cups uncooked red lentil fusilli or wheat pasta *(original recipe: 1 cup)* - 1/2 cup (75g) raw cashews - 2 cups vegetable broth - 2 tablespoons nutritional yeast - 2 tablespoons red miso paste - 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice - 3 1/2 teaspoons [Cajun seasoning](Cajun_seasoning.md.html) - 1 can artichoke hearts - 1 tablespoon capers - 2 soup bowl of fresh spinach ## Preparation 0. Optionally, soak the cashews overnight. 1. Cook the **pasta** according to instructions, with good amount of salt, **just under *al dente***. 2. In blender, combine **cashews**, **broth**, **yeast**, **miso**, **lemon juice**, and **Cajun seasoning**. Blend until smooth. 3. In a large saucepan, combine **sauce**, **cooked pasta**, **artichokes**, and **capers**, and simmer over medium heat. Cook---stirring continuously---until sauce thickens, 5-10 minutes. 4. Once thick enough, stir in the spinach and heat until wilted. Taste and add more salt if needed. Add more seasoning if desired. ## Source - [Vegan Wholesome: High-Protein Meals and Snacks to Energize and Nourish; A Cookbook](https://www.amazon.com/Vegan-Wholesome-High-Protein-Energize-Nourish-ebook/dp/B0D7GJSCVT)
