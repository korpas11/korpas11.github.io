**Red lentil curry** ## Ingredients - 300 g Lentils Masoor Dal - 50 ml oil - 2 tsp cumin Seeds (*Jeera*) - 4 garlic - 1 onions - 2 tomatoes - 1 tsp salt - 1 tsp chilli powder - 1 tsp Turmeric Powder (*Haldi*) - 1 liter water (4 cups) - 2 green chillies - cilantro (coriander) leaves ## Preparation 1. In a pan add the oil and heat up on medium heat until hot 2. Add the **cumin seeds** and finely chopped **garlic** then sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant 3. Add the finely diced **onion** and cook for 4-5 minutes until softened 4. Add the **salt**, **chilli powder**, **turmeric powder**, and finely chopped **green chillies** then cook for 3-4 minutes 5. Add the chopped **tomato** and cook for 3-4 minutes until soft 6. As the tomatoes are cooking wash the **lentils** then cook for 3-4 minutes 7. Add the water then mix before covering and cook for 20 minutes on medium heat until soft 8. Remove lid and cook for 2-3 minutes until thickened to personal preference 9. Serve with homemade chapatti and enjoy! ## Source - [Red Lentil Curry Recipe](https://www.cookwithnabeela.com/recipe/red-lentil-curry/) - [Masoor Dal Recipe - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZSxOiLOUro) ## Comments I needed to add another 1/2 teaspoon of salt.
