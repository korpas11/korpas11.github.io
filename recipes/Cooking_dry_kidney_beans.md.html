**Cooking dry kidney beans** ## Ingredients - 1 pound dried kidney beans (~2 cups) - 10 cups water - 1-2 bay leaves (optional) ## Preparation 1. Cover beans with water and **soak 8 hours** or overnight. 2. Add the soaked, drained beans to a large pot along with **10 cups fresh water** and **bay leaves** (if using). 3. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat to low and simmer. 4. Partially cover pot with lid, leaving a small vent for steam to escape. 5. Cook 45 minutes to 1 1⁄2 hours until beans are tender, stirring occasionally. 6. Check beans at **45 minutes**. They should be tender, creamy, and easy to mash between fingers. 7. Add more cooking time if needed, checking every 10-15 minutes. 8. Remove bay leaves, drain excess liquid as needed. ## Source - [Cooking Dry Kidney Beans](https://thekitchenknowhow.com/how-to-cook-dry-kidney-beans/)
