**Creamy Mexican potato and white bean soup** Very tasty. Serves 6-8 people. ## Ingredients - 1/2 cup raw **cashews** (75g) - 1 cup diced yellow or white **onion** - 6 large **garlic** cloves, minced - 1 teaspoon onion powder - 1 teaspoon garlic powder - 1 teaspoon ground cumin - 1 teaspoon dried oregano - 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika - 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander - 3/4 teaspoon fine sea salt (+ more to taste) - 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper - 4 cups **vegetable broth** (low salt) - *I needed 6 so it not be too thick* - 2 heaping cups gold **potatoes**, 1/2 in cubed (330g) - 4 ounce can of green **chilies** - e.g. poblano pepper - 1 tablespoon finely minced pickled **jalapeño** (10g) - 4 cups [cooked](Cooking_dry_kidney_beans.md.html) **white beans** (2/3 lb uncooked) - 1 cup frozen sweet **corn** - 2 tablespoons fresh **lime** (or lemon) juice (30g) - *diced avocado & red pepper flakes (optional)* ## Preparation 0. Prepare the beans and the cashew by soaking them overnight. 1. In a blender, blend the **cashews** with 1/2 cup water until smooth and creamy 2. In a large pot sauteed the **onion** over oil for 5 minutes. Add the **garlic** and cook for 1 more minute. (Avoid sticking and burning by stirring frequently.) 3. Add the onion powder, garlic powder, cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, coriander, salt, and black pepper and stir. 4. Add the broth, potatoes, chiles, jalapeños and bring to a boil. Stir, and reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until potatoes are almost fully tender, 10-15 minutes. 5. Add the beans, corn, cashew cream, lime juice. Cook until slightly thickened, 5-10 minutes. Taste and add salt if needed. 6. Serve with diced avocado and red pepper flakes, if desired. ## Source - [Vegan Wholesome: High-Protein Meals and Snacks to Energize and Nourish; A Cookbook](https://www.amazon.com/Vegan-Wholesome-High-Protein-Energize-Nourish-ebook/dp/B0D7GJSCVT)
